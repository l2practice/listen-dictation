🧩 Gap-fill
🎧 Listen and fill in the blanks two sentences at a time. Fill every blank in the current pair, then press Enter (or the Check button) — the app grades it instantly and marks each blank ✓ correct / ✗ wrong. If anything is wrong, fix it and press Enter again — your progress on earlier sentences is kept. Once the pair is fully correct, it automatically moves on to the next two sentences.
⚠️ Leaving mid-way loses all progress.
